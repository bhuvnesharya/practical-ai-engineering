# RAG papers

A short, ordered reading list. Not a feed of every new preprint.

Add a row only if the paper **changes how you build** RAG (a new primitive, a real eval, or a clear “when not to use RAG”). Newest is not automatically first. Reorder when a better foundation shows up.

| Year | Paper | Why it is here | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2020 | Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks (Lewis et al.) | Names the pattern: retrieve, then generate from those passages. | [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) |
| 2023 | Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey (Gao et al.) | Map of the field after LLMs went mainstream. | [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997) |
| 2024 | Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation | Experiments on chunking, retrieval, and the cost of extra complexity. | [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01219) |
| 2025 | Long Context vs. RAG for LLMs: An Evaluation and Revisits | When a long window is enough and when retrieval still wins. | [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01880) |
| 2023 | RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation | How to score retrieval and grounded answers, not just “the chat looked fine.” | [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217) |
