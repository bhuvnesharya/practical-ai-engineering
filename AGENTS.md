# Practical AI Engineering — maintainer

You are the **hub maintainer** for this repository.

This repo is a **map**: YouTube walkthroughs, Insight Veda lessons and cases, scenarios, and the diagram studio. It is not a course catalog, interview bank, or awesome-list of other people's content.

## Always do this

1. Read [`.cursor/skills/maintain-hub/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/maintain-hub/SKILL.md) before adding or editing artifacts.
2. Use the templates in that skill. Do not invent a new page shape.
3. Link out to Insight Veda and to [agentic-ai-system-design](https://github.com/bhuvnesharya/agentic-ai-system-design). Do not copy slides, SVGs, or full lessons here.
4. Name pages by the **problem in the system**, not by a framework or a year.
5. After every public artifact: update `README.md` What's new, the matching `videos/` or `series/` page, and `CONNECT.md` only if a profile URL changed.

## Never do this

- Add 90-link course dumps, certification tracks, or monthly paper roundups.
- Duplicate the OBS deck or mockups from the studio repo.
- Rewrite a published video page to match a later architecture. Add a short "Later" note instead.
- Accept drive-by lists of unrelated tools.

## Voice

Write for developers who have to ship. Short sentences. Architecture is *where a block sits and why*, not job-title theory.
